# 🧠 MindPulse | Wellness & Productivity Tracking Platform

> **Live Application:** [mindpulseflow.vercel.app](https://mindpulseflow.vercel.app)  
> **Role:** Product Lead / PM (Concept, Scoping & Deployment)

---

## 📌 Executive Summary
**MindPulse** is a lightweight, low-friction web application designed to bridge the gap between mental wellness and daily habit management. Built around a calm, pastel aesthetic, the platform allows users to log emotional check-ins, run customizable focus blocks, and maintain daily task schedules without cognitive overload.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement & Product Vision
* **The Problem:** Traditional productivity tools are often overly complex, clinical, or overwhelming, leading to user fatigue and high drop-off rates.
* **The Solution:** A whimsical, friction-free dashboard combining mood reflection, structured focus timers, and light habit planning in one unified experience.

---

## ✨ Key Product Features
* 📝 **Daily Mood Check-Ins:** Simple emotional tracking with gentle journal reflections.
* ⏱️ **Focus Engine:** Customizable Pomodoro-style timer blocks designed for deep work.
* 📅 **Habit & Schedule Planner:** Structured daily layout to visualize goals for the week.
* 📊 **Progress Dashboard:** Low-pressure insights to monitor personal growth over time.
